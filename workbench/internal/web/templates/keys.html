Language-model keys

{{with .Data}}

A language model can describe in words what your analyses hear. It works with your own API key for Anthropic or OpenAI: the provider bills your account with them, and the Workbench handles no money. Without a key there is no interpretation. Everything else works without one.

A key is used only for your own analyses, also on sources shared with you, and only while someone is watching. The interpretation itself is not built into the engine yet: until then a saved key is kept, and not used.

How a key is kept

  • Before saving, the Workbench asks the provider once whether the key works, by listing its models. That call costs nothing.
  • The key is stored encrypted. The encryption key is part of the server's settings, not of the database, so a copy of the database alone does not reveal it.
  • After saving, you see only its last four characters. It is never shown again, never written to a log, and never given out through tokens or MCP.
  • Deleting it here deletes it from the Workbench at once. To end it for good, delete it on the provider's page as well.
