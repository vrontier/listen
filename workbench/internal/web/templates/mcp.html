Guide for assistants

A smaller or less capable model may not find its way through the tools on its own. Copy this text into its session first: it says what the Workbench is, in which order to call the tools, and what to do with an error. Every assistant also receives it, without your ids, as the server's instructions when it connects.

Connecting

Make a token with "MCP" ticked under Tokens, and keep it out of files you share: the examples read it from the environment variable LOW_WORKBENCH_TOKEN.

Endpoint{{.Base}}/mcp, MCP over streamable HTTP, header Authorization: Bearer low_…
Claude Code
claude mcp add --transport http low-workbench {{.Base}}/mcp --header "Authorization: Bearer $LOW_WORKBENCH_TOKEN"
.mcp.json
{"mcpServers": {"low-workbench": {"type": "http", "url": "{{.Base}}/mcp",
  "headers": {"Authorization": "Bearer ${LOW_WORKBENCH_TOKEN}"}}}}

The assistant must reach this address: one on your machine or network can, a service in someone's cloud cannot reach an intranet address.
