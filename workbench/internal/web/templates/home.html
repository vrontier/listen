Listening Observatory Workbench

A workspace for researchers who work with live audio streams, such as natural radio at very low frequencies. A stream is registered once and every member can use it. Each member sets up their own analyses: frequency ranges, filter rules and motifs for one source or several compared with each other. A run records what they find as a data set to download, and an AI assistant can help along the way.

Analyses stay private to their member{{if .Data.Publishing}} until they choose to publish them{{end}}.{{if .Research}} Published research is open to everyone.{{end}}

Membership is by invitation. If you have an account, log in with your email address.
