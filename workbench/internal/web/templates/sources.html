Sources

Register a source

A source is a live audio stream: one a member runs, or one whose operator has agreed to its analysis on this Workbench by its members. Sources are shared by all members: every source here can be used in your own analyses, without asking anyone, and your analyses stay your own. Before registering a stream, look whether it is here already; a stream exists once. The Workbench keeps one connection to a source however many analyses use it, and connects only while an analysis that uses it is in a run or a preview.

Whoever registers a source looks after it as its steward: its description and credit, the operator's agreement, and its mode. A source is active unless it is marked otherwise: paused stops it for everyone for a while, retired takes it out of service for good while analyses keep their history, and suspended is the master admin's. A steward may keep a new source to themselves for now and open it to everyone later.
