Register a source

Two steps: the engine checks the stream's address once, then you describe the source and say who allows its analysis here. The address and the sample rate are fixed once saved. A source starts in draft, and connects only while one of your analyses uses it in a run or a preview. If the address is registered already, you are shown whose it is: a stream exists here once, and its owner can share it with you.

Sample rate of the analysis

Fixed for good once saved. The analysis window has 4096 points, so a higher rate reaches higher frequencies but tells close tones apart less well and costs more. 22 050 Hz covers up to about 11 kHz; choose more only if what you study lies higher.
